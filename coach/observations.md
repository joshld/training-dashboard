# Coach Observations

## August 2026

### Running

- Tempo and interval execution is currently a strength.
- Joshua paces quality sessions with good discipline and generally finishes with some reserve.
- Current marathon development should prioritise long-run durability, fueling and maintaining form late in runs rather than chasing faster short-session paces.

### Strength

- Bilateral strength is high, but unilateral endurance work remains useful because running is fundamentally single-leg.
- Slow bodyweight step-ups created substantial quad fatigue after a quality run and full leg session, but both sides were affected similarly and there was no pain.
- Glute bridges up to 90 kg were tolerated without lower-back symptoms.

### Recovery

- Protein intake is generally more than sufficient.
- Carbohydrate intake, hydration and sleep deserve more attention after combined quality-running and leg-training days.
- The recent lower-back tweak is improving, but loaded squats should return progressively rather than abruptly.

### Coaching Approach

- Preserve user control and explain recommendations clearly.
- Avoid treating one workout as definitive evidence of weakness, fitness or race capability.
- Keep the dashboard focused on useful trends and decisions rather than collecting every possible metric.
