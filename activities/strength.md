## Current Lower-Body Guidance

- Delay heavy barbell squats until the back remains symptom-free under progressive reloading.
- Use bodyweight squats, machine isolation, glute bridges and controlled unilateral work to maintain training stimulus.
- Step-up fatigue after a quality run and full leg session does not by itself prove weakness or poor squat technique.
- When squats return, use video from the side and front-quarter angle to assess bar path, bracing, depth, knee tracking and left/right shift.

## Progression Priorities

1. Maintain pain-free hip extension and unilateral control.
2. Reintroduce loaded squats at moderate effort before returning to heavy work.
3. Track exercise load, sets, reps and RPE consistently.
4. Preserve at least one genuinely lower-fatigue day between major lower-body stressors where possible.
