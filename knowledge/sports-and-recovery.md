# Sport and Recovery Library

## Indoor Futsal

**ID:** `sport-futsal`

**Purpose:** Team sport, enjoyment, agility and repeated high-intensity running.

**Load profile:** Treat as a quality running session rather than easy cross-training. Repeated acceleration, deceleration and change of direction can create substantial calf, hamstring, knee and adductor load.

**Typical duration:** 45–120 minutes.

**Recovery demand:** Moderate to high.

**Planning guidance:** Avoid stacking with hard intervals or heavy lower-body strength immediately before or after when possible. A long run followed by futsal may be tolerated by an experienced athlete but should be treated as a demanding combined day.

**Tags:** `sport`, `soccer`, `futsal`, `quality`, `high-impact`

## Outdoor Soccer Match

**ID:** `sport-soccer-match`

**Purpose:** Competitive match play that develops soccer-specific fitness through repeated high-intensity efforts, sprinting, change of direction and sustained aerobic work.

**Load profile:** High variability with sprinting, collisions and large total movement volume.

**Typical duration:** 60–120 minutes including warm-up and stoppages.

**Recovery demand:** High.

**Planning guidance:** Usually replace, rather than add to, another hard running session that week. Avoid placing immediately before a key long run, race or heavy lower-body strength session where possible.

**Tags:** `sport`, `soccer`, `quality`, `high-impact`

## Easy Cycling

**ID:** `cross-training-easy-cycle`

**Purpose:** Low-impact aerobic work or active recovery.

**Intensity:** RPE 2–4.

**Recovery demand:** Low, unless duration or hills are substantial.

**Tags:** `cross-training`, `cycling`, `aerobic`, `low-impact`

---

## Recovery Walk

**ID:** `recovery-walk`

**Purpose:** Gentle movement, circulation and mental recovery.

**Intensity:** Comfortable.

**Typical duration:** 15–60 minutes.

**Recovery demand:** Very low.

**Tags:** `recovery`, `walking`, `low-fatigue`

---

## Mobility Session

**ID:** `recovery-mobility`

**Purpose:** Maintain comfortable range of motion and address stiffness without forcing end range.

**Typical duration:** 10–30 minutes.

**Recovery demand:** Very low.

**Guidance:** Mobility is supportive work, not a substitute for evaluating persistent or worsening pain.

**Tags:** `recovery`, `mobility`, `low-fatigue`
